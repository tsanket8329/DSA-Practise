Problem: collections.Counter()

A shoe shop owner has X shoes in stock. The sizes of the shoes are given in a list. There are N customers. Each customer wants to buy one shoe of a specific size and is willing to pay a certain price for it.

A customer can buy a shoe only if the requested size is available in stock. Once a shoe is sold, it is removed from the inventory.

Your task is to calculate the total amount of money earned by the shoe shop owner.

Input Format
The first line contains an integer X, the number of shoes.
The second line contains X space-separated integers representing the shoe sizes in stock.
The third line contains an integer N, the number of customers.
The next N lines each contain two integers:
size – the shoe size desired by the customer.
price – the amount the customer will pay for that shoe.
Output Format

Print a single integer representing the total money earned.

Sample Input
10
2 3 4 5 6 8 7 6 5 18
6
6 55
6 45
6 55
4 40
18 60
10 50
Sample Output
200
Explanation
Customer 1 buys size 6 for 55.
Customer 2 buys another size 6 for 45.
Customer 3 wants size 6, but none are left.
Customer 4 buys size 4 for 40.
Customer 5 buys size 18 for 60.
Customer 6 wants size 10, which is not available.

Total earnings = 55 + 45 + 40 + 60 = 200.
CODE
from collections import Counter

n = int(input())
shoes = list(map(int, input().split()))
stock = Counter(shoes)

customers = int(input())
total = 0

for _ in range(customers):
    size, price = map(int, input().split())
    if stock[size] > 0:
        total += price
        stock[size] -= 1

print(total)
